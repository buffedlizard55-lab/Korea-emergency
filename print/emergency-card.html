Rules that can stop you at the border

  • e-Arrival Card — free, required. File within 3 days before arrival; lapses 72 h after submitting. Only e-arrivalcard.go.kr is official — any site asking for payment is fake.
  • Cash over USD 10,000 combined must be declared on arrival. The certificate cannot be issued once you leave the arrivals area.
  • Never carry cannabis / CBD / hemp, in any form, even in transit. Narcotics and stimulants need MFDS approval in advance.
  • No visa needed ≤ 90 days · no K-ETA through 31 Dec 2026 · passport must be valid at entry.
