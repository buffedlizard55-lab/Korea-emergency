Read these five notes first

These are the biggest trip-planning flags already documented in the guide.

  1. e-Arrival Card is mandatory

    U.S. citizens are listed as K-ETA exempt through 31 Dec 2026, but an arrival card is still required if entering without K-ETA. Read entry details.

  2. Arrival is late at night

    Check current AREX or night-bus options. Taxi fares and surcharges vary by taxi type, time, route, tolls, and official fare rules. Read transport notes.

  3. CSAT is Thu 19 Nov

    The exam falls during the Busan stay. KICE lists English as 13:10–14:20 and says listening starts at 13:10. Read date notes.

  4. No national public holidays are listed for 1–22 Nov

    The current VISITKOREA calendar shows no national public holidays in the travel window, but local events and schedules still need checking. Review holidays and traffic.

  5. Medication rules need care

    No cannabis/CBD. Controlled medicines need MFDS approval; non-controlled medicines should travel with original prescription and doctor letter. Read health and medication notes.
