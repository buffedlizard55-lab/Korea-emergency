If something is happening right now

Tap a card to call. On 112 or 119, say “English please” — you will be connected to an interpreter. Both numbers work from any phone without an area code, and are free from public payphones. Source: Busan Metropolitan City — Emergency Rescue Call 112/119

119 Ambulance · Fire · Rescue Also sea & coast rescue. Interpreters available; your location shows automatically. 112 Police Crime, theft, assault, feeling unsafe. Free interpretation at stations. 1330 Travel Helpline — English 24/7 Interpreting, tourist help, complaints. From overseas +82-2-1330. +82-2-397-4114 U.S. Embassy Seoul — 24/7 Lost passport, arrest, death, serious emergency. From the U.S. & Canada: +1-888-407-4747.
Can’t speak, or can’t describe the situation? 119 accepts reports four other ways: the “119 신고” app (Play Store / App Store), a text message (SMS or MMS with photos/video) sent to 119, a video call (dial 119, tap the video-call button), or online at 119.go.kr. Five things that trip travelers up

All verified against the operating agency’s own page.

⚠️ 1339 is NOT an ambulance

1339 is the KDCA infectious-disease line (24/7). Some tourism pages still call it an “emergency medical information center” — that wording is stale. For an ambulance, call 119.

KDCA · VISITKOREA

⚠️ 122 no longer exists

The old maritime emergency number was merged into 119. At Haeundae, Gwangalli or Songdo: someone in the water → 119. Don’t go in after them.

MOIS — integrated emergency reporting · Official AIP: sea rescue = 119

⚠️ lost112.go.kr is dead

The national police lost-property site was merged into 경찰민원24 on 26 Jan 2026. Search and report lost property at minwon24.police.go.kr (Korean — use translation or 1330). Official metro/rail pages still print the old address.

minwon24.police.go.kr · AREX merger notice

⚠️ Fake e-Arrival Card sites

The official portal warns that copycat sites exist. e-arrivalcard.go.kr is the ONLY official site; any site asking for payment is fake. The real card is free.

Official portal warning

⚠️ Declare cash over USD 10,000

More than USD 10,000 in means of payment (foreign currency, KRW notes or checks, combined) must be declared at Customs — tick item 3 on the traveler declaration form and get the certificate before leaving the arrivals area. It cannot be issued later.

Korea Customs Service

✅ No Korean public holidays during your trip

Korea has 14 public holidays in 2026 and none falls in November, so no holiday-driven mass travel. CSAT (19 Nov) is not a public holiday — it is a normal working day.

Before you fly

The few things that can actually stop you at the border — and the daily basics.

📄 e-Arrival Card — required

You travel on the K-ETA exemption (through 31 Dec 2026), so you must submit the e-Arrival Card. Free. Filing opens 3 days before arrival (Korea time) and a submission lapses 72 hours after you send it — don’t file too early.

Official portal only

e-arrivalcard.go.kr · K-ETA exemption (State Dept)

🛂 Entry, customs & meds

  • No visa ≤ 90 days (tourism).
  • Passport valid at entry — Korea sets no minimum validity.
  • Declare payment means over USD 10,000 (item 3 on the form).
  • Never carry cannabis/CBD. Narcotics, opioids & amphetamines need K-MFDS pre-approval; even poppy-seed products are illegal.
  • Non-controlled meds: original containers + prescription; check the rules doc.

State Dept · Korea Customs · U.S. Embassy — controlled substances

💉 Health basics (CDC)

  • Be current on routine vaccines + MMR (global measles notice) and COVID-19.
  • Hepatitis A recommended if unvaccinated; Hep B if unvaccinated & under 60.
  • No malaria risk in Seoul or Busan — risk is rural northern Incheon/Gangwon/Gyeonggi (Mar–Dec). JE vaccine not recommended for short urban trips.
  • You pay for treatment in Korea — the State Dept recommends insurance incl. medical evacuation.

CDC — South Korea · State Dept (insurance)

If something happens

Short playbook rows — full protocols in the scenario doc.

SituationDo this
Medical emergency Call 119, say “English please.” Interpreters connect; your location is transmitted automatically.
Lost / stolen passport Police report at the nearest station (or 112) → U.S. Embassy Seoul +82-2-397-4114 → appointment with DS-11 + statement, photo, police report, $165 paid online. Busan cannot issue passports.
Crime / theft / unsafe 112 or any police station (free interpretation in emergencies). Tourist complaints/overcharging: 1330 or touristcomplaint.or.kr.
Can’t speak / silent reporting Text (SMS/MMS) to 119, video-call 119, “119 신고” app, Baro app, or 119.go.kr online.
Earthquake Shaking usually passes in 1–2 minutes: under a sturdy table, protect your head; then secure an exit, shut gas & power. Expect Korean-language alerts — open Emergency Ready for English.
Someone in the water 119 — not 122 (gone). Don’t enter the water yourself.
Disaster alert on your phone Phone alerts are mostly Korean. Emergency Ready App shows alert texts in English, Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai or Japanese; Safe Korea portal carries the live feeds.
Mental-health crisis Call 109 — 24/7 and confidential (Korean-language). In immediate danger, 119. U.S. citizen emergencies (either traveler): +82-2-397-4114, 24/7.
