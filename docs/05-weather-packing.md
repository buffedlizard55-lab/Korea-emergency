# 5. Weather & Packing

Source: Korea Meteorological Administration forecast and warning services [S12];
fine-dust information: AirKorea [S13]. No fixed forecast is asserted here.

## November weather (Seoul)
| Metric | Official verification |
|--------|-----------------------|
| Temperature, precipitation, snow | Check the KMA forecast and warnings close to each travel date [S12]. |
| Fine dust | Check AirKorea close to each travel date [S13]. |

- **It's colder than it looks.** Evenings and windy days feel much colder.
- By **mid–late November** it gets noticeably colder; northern/eastern areas
  (Gangwon) and higher ground can see light snow.
- Do not rely on a city-wide average; check the destination-specific forecast.

## Packing list (layering is key)
**Clothing**
- Thermal base layers (top + bottom)
- Mid-layer (fleece / sweater / knit)
- Warm outer layer (medium-weight down or insulated jacket) — essential for
  evenings
- Trousers / lined jeans; a warm pair for cold days
- Scarf, gloves, beanie/hat (windchill protection)
- Comfortable, broken-in walking shoes; a waterproof/resistant pair for rain
- A couple of nice outfits for dinners/temples (modest, covered shoulders
  for temples)

**Health & comfort**
- KF94 / N95 masks (fine-dust + crowds) — [S13]
- Basic first-aid kit: bandages, antiseptic wipes, blister plasters,
  pain reliever, anti-diarrheal, rehydration salts, antihistamine
- Personal Rx meds + doctor's letters (see `04`)
- Lip balm & hand cream (cold, dry air)
- Sunscreen (sunny days; UV still moderate)
- Small hand sanitizer

**Electronics**
- Universal travel adapter (Korea uses **Type C / Type F** plugs, **220V**,
  60 Hz) + voltage converter **only** if you have non-dual-voltage devices
  (most phone chargers/laptops are 100–240V; check labels)
- Portable power bank
- Phone data: eSIM or travel SIM is easiest; or rent pocket Wi-Fi at ICN
- Offline maps (download Google Maps offline / Naver Map / Kakao Map in
  English) — Google Maps is limited in Korea; **Naver Map / Kakao Map** work
  better. Download offline areas before you go.

**Money & security**
- A debit/credit card (tap/EMV); **T-money card** for transit (see `09`)
- Some cash (KRW) for small vendors/taxis
- RFID-blocking wallet/passport holder

**Documents** — see `02-entry-documents.md`.

## What NOT to pack
- ❌ Cannabis/CBD/hemp products (any form, incl. edibles/oils) — see `04`
- ❌ Narcotic/controlled medications without MFDS approval
- ❌ Fresh fruits, meats, produce subject to customs restrictions (declare if
  unsure)
- ❌ Non-dual-voltage appliances without a converter (they'll burn out)
