## V. U.S. Embassy — arrest, lawyers, death, emergency passport

| # | Entry | What the official source says | Official link | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **N104** | **If a U.S. citizen is arrested — what ACS will do** | Embassy page dated **14 Apr 2026**: ask prison authorities to **notify the U.S. embassy or consulate**, or call the Embassy yourself. ACS **can**: provide a list of English-speaking local attorneys; contact family/friends/employer **with written permission**; visit the detainee regularly; help ensure appropriate medical care; give a general overview of the local criminal-justice process; point to victim resources. Footer emergency number: **(02)-397-4114**. Verify credentials via the **Korean Bar Association** search (Korean) or KBA’s English how-to. KOTRA also publishes English-speaking lawyers. Embassy **cannot** act as legal representative or give legal advice (FAQ, 13 Apr 2026). | [kr.usembassy.gov — Legal Assistance](https://kr.usembassy.gov/services-attorneys/) · [Lawyers PDF](https://kr.usembassy.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/67/2026/07/List-of-Lawyers.pdf) · [KBA English how-to](https://www.koreanbar.or.kr/eng/pages/news/view.asp?page=1&seq=13077&types=201) | Verified (page read) |
| **N106** | **Death of a U.S. citizen — CRODA by email, no walk-in** | Embassy page dated **16 Apr 2026**: next of kin email **SeoulinfoACS@state.gov**. Normal processing **1–3 business days** after all documents; status pings inside that window **delay** the file. Required: deceased’s passport (then **mail the original** to ACS, **188 Sejong-daero, Jongno-gu, Seoul 03141** — **no in-person appointment** for this); hospital death certificate (EN or Korean hangul OK, **no notarized translation required**); next-of-kin ID; proof of relationship; disposition of remains. **Cremation letter** (often required for U.S. citizens without ARC/residency/visa): request by email; some crematoria want a hard copy — then email for an **emergency appointment**. **State has no funds** to return remains. Morticians list (Apr 2026 PDF) is on the same page. | [kr.usembassy.gov — Death of a U.S. Citizen](https://kr.usembassy.gov/services-death-of-a-u-s-citizen/) · [Morticians PDF](https://kr.usembassy.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/67/2026/04/Morticians.pdf) | Verified (page read) |
| **N107** | **Emergency (limited-validity) passport — same-day print, 1 year, not always accepted** | Embassy page dated **30 July 2026**: an emergency passport is **limited to one year or less** and **has no electronic chip**. **Some countries / airlines may refuse it.** Normally **printed the same day**. **Passport cards cannot** be issued with an emergency passport. Book an in-person appointment to explain urgent travel. If travel is **within 5 working days** and no slots exist, email **support-acs-southkorea@usvisascheduling.com** with subject **“Emergency Passport Request”** plus itinerary, name, DOB, local phone, three preferred 09:00 dates, and reason. Appointments are **not guaranteed**. Documents/fees match a regular passport application. | [kr.usembassy.gov — Emergency Passport](https://kr.usembassy.gov/services-emergency-passport-replacing-an-emergency-passport/) | Verified (page read) |
| **N108** | **Embassy FAQ (13 Apr 2026) — Busan cannot do passports; notarial is never an emergency** | Official FAQ: **all routine services require an appointment.** **No consular services at the Busan Consulate** — passport / CRBA / notary only in **Seoul**. **Notarial needs are not considered an emergency**; walk-ins for notarials are **refused entry**. Regular full-validity passports take **about 4 weeks** (printed in the U.S.). Emergency limited passports can be printed **the same day**. Forgotten appointment passwords **cannot** be retrieved. | [kr.usembassy.gov — FAQ](https://kr.usembassy.gov/services-faq/) | Verified (page read) |
