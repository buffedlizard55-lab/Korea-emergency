# 1. Trip Overview

## Who & what
- **Travelers:** 2 U.S. citizens, 1 male + 1 female, residents of California.
- **Destination:** South Korea (arriving Seoul via Incheon International
  Airport, ICN).
- **Trip length:** 31 Oct 2026 – 22 Nov 2026.

| Event | Date | Day of week |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Depart SFO | 31 Oct 2026 | Saturday |
| Arrive Incheon (ICN), **9:00 PM local** | 1 Nov 2026 | Sunday |

## Key takeaways for this trip
1. **You arrive late (9 PM) on a Sunday.** Check the official AREX timetable and Incheon Airport night-bus page. If you
   clear immigration late, plan for a current night-bus option or taxi. See `07-transportation.md`.
2. **No K-ETA needed** for your 2026 dates — the exemption runs through 31
   Dec 2026. **But you must submit the free e-Arrival Card** during the
   official pre-arrival window. See `02-entry-documents.md`.
3. **No Korean public holidays** fall during your trip (1–22 Nov). The **CSAT
   exam on 19 Nov** can still affect travel and schedules, but exact traffic,
   office/bank, road, and aviation changes must be checked against dated local
   notices. KICE lists English from 13:10–14:20 and says listening starts at
   13:10. See `06-holidays-traffic-dates.md`.
4. **Travel advisory is Level 1** (lowest): exercise normal precautions.
   Enroll in **STEP** before you go. See `08-safety-security.md`.
5. **Weather varies by city and date** — check the official KMA forecast close to departure. Pack layers.
   See `05-weather-packing.md`.

## Time zone & jet lag
- South Korea is **Korea Standard Time (UTC+9)**, 16 hours ahead of Pacific
  Daylight Time (UTC–7) in late Oct/early Nov, then 17 hours ahead after the
  U.S. falls back on **1 Nov 2026** (PDT → PST).
- You arrive 9 PM local on 1 Nov after flying from California. Plan a relaxed
  first morning and use daylight to reset.
