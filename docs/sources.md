## Key facts and the source that confirms them

- **Travel Advisory level:** Level 1 — *Exercise normal precautions.* [S1]
- **K-ETA:** Exemption for U.S. passport holders **extended through 31 Dec
  2026**. Your 2026 trip therefore does **not** require a K-ETA. Requirement
  resumes 1 Jan 2027. [S1][S8]
- **e-Arrival Card:** The official guide says foreigners entering Korea can fill
  out the e-Arrival Card online; filing is available starting 3 days before
  arrival in Korea based on Korean Standard Time; the card is free; and validity
  is automatically lost after 72 hours from submission. K-ETA materials state
  K-ETA holders do not need to submit an arrival card, so K-ETA-exempt travelers
  should use the arrival-card process. [S7][S8]
- **Visa:** Not required for tourism/business stays ≤ 90 days. [S1]
- **Emergency numbers:** 112 police, 119 ambulance/fire, 1366 domestic
  violence hotline, +82-2-3210-0404 international caller emergency center,
  U.S. Embassy +82-2-397-4114 (24/7). [S1]
- **Korea Travel Helpline:** 1330 / +82-2-1330 provides travel assistance;
  Korea.net describes 1330 Korea Travel Hotline service as available 365 days a year, 24 hours a day. [S3]
- **CSAT (수능) 2026 exam:** KICE materials identify the 2027 CSAT test date as
  Thursday **19 November 2026**. KICE's CSAT schedule page lists English from
  13:10–14:20 and says the listening test starts at 13:10 for about 25 minutes.
  This falls *inside* your trip window. [S10]
- **No Korean national public holidays** fall between 1 Nov and 22 Nov 2026
  (full 2026 official holiday calendar). [S9]
- **Weather:** Use KMA forecasts and warnings for destination-specific, date-specific conditions. [S12]
- **Vaccines (CDC):** be current on routine vaccines; CDC recommends Hepatitis A
  and Typhoid for most travelers; Japanese encephalitis vaccination depends on
  itinerary, duration, rural/outdoor exposure, and medical advice. [S6]
- **Medication import:** narcotics/opiates/amphetamines/benzodiazepines and
  CBD/marijuana/hemp are heavily restricted or prohibited; non-controlled
  prescription meds allowed up to ~6 bottles / 3-month supply with original
  prescription + doctor's letter. [S2][S4][S11]
