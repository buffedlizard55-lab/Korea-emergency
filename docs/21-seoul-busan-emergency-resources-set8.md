## AD. Weather warnings, earthquake action, silent 112

| # | Entry | What the official source says | Official link | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **N148** | **KMA English — what “advisory” vs “warning” actually means (Nov-relevant)** | Live KMA `/neng` **Current Severe Weather Status** table. **Cold wave (Oct–Apr)** advisory: morning min drops **≥10 °C** from the previous day to **≤3 °C** and is **≥3 °C below seasonal average**, **or** morning min **≤ –12 °C for ≥2 days**, **or** significant damage expected from a rapid drop. Warning uses **15 °C drop / –15 °C / widespread damage**. **Heavy snowfall** advisory = **5 cm / 24 h**; warning = **20 cm / 24 h** (mountains 30 cm). **Wind wave** advisory = sea winds **≥14 m/s for ≥3 h** or significant wave **≥3 m**; warning = **21 m/s / 5 m**. **Asian dust** has **no advisory row** — warning only when hourly average **PM10 ≥ 800 μg/m³ for ≥2 hours**. ## AE. Embassy paper that is *not* an emergency (but can become one)

| # | Entry | What the official source says | Official link | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **N151** | **Marriage / divorce in Korea — Embassy limits + MOFA apostille 02-2002-0251** | Embassy FAQ dated **13 Apr 2026**. A Korea-law marriage is generally valid in all U.S. states; Embassy **keeps no marriage records**. Korean marriage-certificate apostille: **MOFA 02-2002-0251 / 0252**. Proof of being unmarried to marry in Korea = **notarial affidavit, $50**, appointment only. Copies after **31 Jan 1995** = the **ward office** that registered it; before that date Seoul City Hall Civil Service **+82-2-2133-7903** (and Dasan **+82-2-731-2120**). **Embassy will not file a divorce** and you need not report one; use the lawyers list (N105). **ROK does not recognize same-sex marriages**, including those valid in the U.S. | [kr.usembassy.gov — FAQ (Marriage/Divorce)](https://kr.usembassy.gov/services-faq/) | Verified (page read) |
| **N152** | **Dual U.S./ROK citizens — U.S. passport required to enter/leave the U.S.** | Same FAQ. Staying in Korea a long time does **not** by itself lose U.S. citizenship (renunciation is in person at the Embassy after age 18). **22 CFR 53.1:** a U.S. citizen may not enter or depart the United States **without a valid U.S. passport** (unless excepted). A Korean passport is **not** a substitute. Ticket-name mismatches: ask the airline and **DHS/CBP at the U.S. port of entry**. Male ROK nationals, **including duals**, have military-service duties; choose nationality by **31 March of the year they turn 18** or they remain subject. Ages **25–37** who have not served need an **MMA overseas-travel permit**. Official pointer: **mma.go.kr**. Not your two travelers unless one is a dual national — kept because a companion / relative situation comes up. | [kr.usembassy.gov — FAQ (Dual Citizens)](https://kr.usembassy.gov/services-faq/) | Verified (page read) |
| **N153** | **Embassy cannot fetch or translate Korean vital records** | Embassy **Obtaining Vital Records** page (3 Sep 2024, still live): Embassy **keeps no files** of CRBAs or death reports it issues (those go to Washington) and **no Korean civil documents**. **“The Embassy cannot obtain documents on your behalf, and cannot provide translations of documents issued in Korean.”** Embassies **do not perform marriages**. Post-Feb 1995 copies = the registering ward office (Jongno-gu nearest the Embassy: Citizen Affairs, 43 Sambong-ro, **011-82-2-2148-1934** from the U.S.). Complements N138 (no apostille) and N151. | [kr.usembassy.gov — Obtaining Vital Records](https://kr.usembassy.gov/services-obtaining-vital-records-and-other-legal-documents/) | Verified (page read) |
| **N154** | **Embassy will not answer Korean visa / U.S. CBP / “can I bring this medicine” on the fly** | Same FAQ, Service Fees/Other. **Korean admission, stay length, layover visa, visa change/extension = Korean Immigration 1345 / immigration.go.kr** — “the U.S. Embassy has no authority.” **U.S. entry / food / agriculture / customs on the way home:** DHS/CBP at the U.S. port of entry; in Korea you may email **SeoulCBP@state.gov**. **Bringing prescription drugs into Korea:** confirm with MFDS **Narcotics@korea.kr** (Narcotics Policy Division) before you fly — do not ask ACS to clear a pill at the airport. Complements N7 / N76 / docs/04. | [kr.usembassy.gov — FAQ (Other)](https://kr.usembassy.gov/services-faq/) | Verified (page read) |
