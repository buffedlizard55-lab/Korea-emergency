## Phase 1 — Seoul, 1–9 Nov (8 nights)

### Sun 1 Nov — arrival, ICN 21:00
1. **Immigration:** passport + **e-Arrival Card** confirmation. Submit within the official window —
   filing opens **3 days before arrival (KST)**, is **free**, and lapses **72 h after submission**.
   Only `www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr` is official; **any site asking for payment is fake** (N37).
   No visa needed ≤90 days; **no K-ETA** through 31 Dec 2026.
2. **Customs:** carrying over **USD 10,000** combined? You must declare — tick item 3 on the Traveler
   Declaration Form and get the certificate **before leaving the immigration area** (N35).
   Never carry cannabis/CBD in any form.
3. **Transport into Seoul:** check the live AREX timetable; otherwise airport night bus or taxi.
   Use Kakao T or the meter. Have the hotel address **in Korean**.
4. **Before bed:** save 112 / 119 / 1330 / **+82-2-397-4114**; message your CA contact.

### 2–8 Nov — Seoul days
- **Every morning:** KMA English forecast ([kma.go.kr/neng](https://www.kma.go.kr/neng/index.do), N28)
  and AirKorea for fine dust. Pack KF94/N95 if AQI is poor.
- **Weekend nights (7–8 Nov):** Itaewon / Hongdae get dense. If compressed, move to an edge or exit;
  avoid bottlenecked alleys. Call **112** if unsafe.
- **Tourist police** patrol Myeong-dong, Itaewon and Dongdaemun — **dial 1330** to locate the nearest
  officer. Police stations provide **free interpretation** in an emergency (N34).
- **Taxis:** confirm the meter is running. **Call vans have no meter — agree the fare first** (N34).
  Overcharged? Report to **1330** / touristcomplaint.or.kr (N23).
- **Consular:** U.S. Embassy, 188 Sejong-daero, Jongno-gu — closed weekends/holidays; the
  **24/7 emergency line is +82-2-397-4114**.

## Phase 2 — Busan, 9–15 Nov (6 nights)

### Mon 9 Nov — Seoul → Busan
Book KTX through Korail's official global site or Korail Talk (N40). Check live times — do not rely
on static figures.

### Busan standing flags
- **🔴 No U.S. consular services in Busan.** State Dept: consular services, including passports, are
  **only at the U.S. Embassy in Seoul.** A lost passport here means returning to Seoul. Keep
  passport photocopies split between the two of you.
- **Emergency:** 112 / 119 work without an area code and are **free from payphones**; Busan notes the
  computer-dispatched lines are **faster than walking into a police office** (N24). For 119 give
  **exact location, type of incident, and your phone number**.
- **119 interpretation:** Visit Busan states 119 provides translation in **17 languages** and that
  calling 119 auto-locates you (N13).
- **Hospitals:** **Dong-A University Hospital** (26 Daesingongwon-ro, Seo-gu, +82-51-240-2000) has a
  **24-hour emergency medical centre**. **Pusan National University Hospital** (+82-51-240-7000) has
  multilingual translation. Near Haeundae: **Inje University Haeundae Paik Hospital**
  (875 Haeun-daero, +82-51-797-0566) — weekday international desk (N14/N15).
- **Pharmacies:** Busan's own English pharmacy page, plus pharm114 for late-night duty pharmacies.
- **Coast (Haeundae / Gwangalli / Songdo):** November is **outside the lifeguarded swimming season** —
  no supervision. Read Busan's official **"Safety in water"** guide (N26). If someone is in trouble,
  call **119** — **122 no longer exists.** Do not enter the water yourself.
- **High-rise stays:** check the **Apartment Fire Evacuation Manual** (N27); locate two exits.
- **Earthquake:** Busan publishes guidelines in **12 languages** (N19) — shaking is "usually over
  within one or two minutes"; shelter under a sturdy table, protect your head, secure an exit, turn
  off gas/electricity. Verify any tremor on the KMA earthquake database (N38).
- **Help lines:** Busan 120 (weekdays 09:00–18:00, 6 languages) · **1600-0051** Foreign Residents Call
  Center (9 languages, Sun–Fri) · Danuri Busan **051-508-1366**.
- **Metro lost item:** Humetro lost & found, **Seomyeon Station**, 09:00–18:00 Mon–Fri,
  **051-640-7339**. Items move on quickly — report the same day (N20).

## Phase 3 — Seoul, 15–22 Nov (7 nights) — includes CSAT

### Sun 15 Nov — Busan → Seoul
Sunday return trains are popular; reserve early.

### ⚠️ Thu 19 Nov — CSAT (수능) — **a Seoul day for you**

**Confirmed timetable** (KICE guideline PDF / KICE notice 제2026-85호):

| Session | Time |
|---|---|
| Candidate entry complete | **by 08:10** |
| 1 Korean | 08:40–10:00 |
| 2 Mathematics | 10:30–12:10 |
| **3 English** | **13:10–14:20** (listening: 17 items **within 25 min from 13:10**) |
| 4 Korean History + Social/Science/Vocational | 14:50–16:37 |
| 5 Second foreign language / Hanja | 17:05–17:45 |

**What this means in Seoul:**
- Disruption is **not limited to the English hour** — the day runs roughly **08:10 to 17:45**.
- **Morning rush is the worst window.** Test-takers are converging on schools before 08:10; police
  assist late candidates. Avoid time-critical cross-city journeys early morning.
- Use **metro**, not a car or taxi, near schools. Do not park near test centres.
- **Evening:** restaurants get busy after the exam ends; book if you want a specific place.
- ⚠️ **Do not assume a flight hold.** The often-repeated "13:05–13:40 nationwide takeoff/landing
  ban" appears only in unofficial sources. If you have any flight that day, verify with the airline
  and airport directly.
- CSAT is **not a public holiday** — banks and offices operate, but check individual venue hours.

### Sat 21 Nov — last full day
Weekend crowds; keep documents/meds/emergency cards in carry-on.

### Sun 22 Nov — departure
Allow a generous buffer to ICN plus the airline's check-in recommendation. Carry passports, boarding
passes, and a copy of both travelers' documents.
